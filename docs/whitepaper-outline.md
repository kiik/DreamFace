# Whitepaper outline

Working outline for a future research paper. Sections are placeholders until supported by literature review, prototype evidence, and evaluation.

1. **Abstract** — Problem, approach, main evidence, limitations.
2. **Motivation** — Context-window pressure and the need for inspectable agentic-loop control.
3. **Research questions and contributions** — State what is novel and testable.
4. **Background and related work** — Conversation memory, segmentation, retrieval/compression, workflow control, and agentic terminal extensions; include treebank methodology (Penn Treebank, Universal Dependencies), discourse treebanks (RST-DT, PDTB), and dialogue-act corpora (SWBD-DAMSL, ISO 24617-2).
5. **Conceptual model** — Segment granularity, function labels, provenance, relationships, and retention decisions.
6. **Control model** — Plan, Build, Auto, and Horde mode semantics and work-item decomposition.
7. **Prototype and integration boundary** — OpenCode extension points, custom functions, and implementation constraints.
8. **Corpus and annotation (context treebank)** — Construction protocol: language coverage, sampling, permissions, schema, annotator training, agreement, releases, and validation.
9. **Evaluation design** — Tasks, baselines, measures, ablations, and reproducibility.
10. **Results** — Report outcomes, uncertainty, and negative results.
11. **Discussion** — Trade-offs, failure modes, human control, and generalizability.
12. **Limitations and ethics** — Privacy, representativeness, language coverage, and deployment boundaries.
13. **Conclusion and future work**
14. **References and appendices** — RFCs, annotation guide, experiment materials, and terminology register.

## Evidence ledger

For every claim in the paper, record whether it is a literature claim, design rationale, hypothesis, or empirical result, and link it to a source or experiment. Do not report a design proposal as a demonstrated result.
